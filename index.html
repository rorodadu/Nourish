Common questions

Do diners need to install an app?

No. Scanning the table's QR code opens the menu in the phone's browser, with no account or download.

What happens when a dish sells out?

The restaurant switches it off once and it greys out on every diner's screen right away.

How accurate are the calories?

Restaurants enter their own values, so they are estimates. They do not replace advice from a doctor or dietitian.

How do customers pay?

For now, by cash or card at the table. Diners choose when they order and the staff mark it as paid.
