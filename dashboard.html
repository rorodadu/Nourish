Restaurant dashboard

Getting started

Three quick steps and you are ready for your first customers.

    Orders

    New orders from your customers appear here by themselves. Press Start cooking when you begin, then Done when the food is ready. The customer sees each step on their phone.

    Tables and QR codes

    Each table gets its own QR code. Diners scan it and your menu opens with their table number already attached.

    Table availability

    Diners who didn't scan a table's QR code pick from the tables marked Available. A table also becomes Taken when a diner sends an order, so press Free up when they leave.

    Menu

    Add your food and drinks here. Start with a category (like Starters), then add dishes. Only the name, category and price are required. Everything else is optional.
